Menzies Aviation Launches Refugee Training Program in Uganda

Aviation Pros · 8 Oct 2026

Menzies Aviation has partnered with Amahoro Coalition to launch a Refugee Workforce Program in Uganda, providing aviation training, skills development and potential employment opportunities for displaced individuals.

The first cohort of 12 participants began training this week at the Menzies Aviation Training School. Uganda is the first country in Menzies' Middle East, Africa and Asia region to introduce the initiative, with plans to expand the program to other locations.

Following an initial assessment, participants will pursue either the IATA Airline Customer Service Diploma or the IATA Cargo Introductory Diploma, gaining industry-recognized qualifications relevant to airport and cargo operations.

‘Interoperability Not Truly Free’: Fintech Experts On West Africa’s Instant-Payments Rollout

Forbes Africa · 8 Oct 2026

The move by Central Bank of West Africa’s eight-nation monetary union is designed to accelerate digital payments but the debate is on how the costs will be shared across the industry.

The Central Bank of West African States (BCEAO) said early October that interoperable electronic-money transactions within the West African Economic and Monetary Union (UEMOA) must be processed through its Interoperable Platform of the Instant Payment System, known as PI-SPI, from November 2.

Under the new rules, domestic transfers sent by an individual will be free when the user’s cumulative daily transfers through a participant are 8,000 CFA francs ($13.66) or less, regardless of the number of transactions. BCEAO said that threshold covers 75% of electronic-money transactions in the region.
