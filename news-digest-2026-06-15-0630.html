Education has contributed to around half of global economic growth and, since 1980, to a 40% reduction in extreme poverty. However, continued underinvestment in education has resulted in an estimated annual education financing gap of around $97 billion in lower- and middle-income countries.

Multilateral cooperation – once the integral framework for global development – is becoming increasingly strained as geopolitical shifts have led to widespread cuts to international aid. As a result, providing quality education for all children is increasingly viewed as a second-tier priority. By 2026, it’s estimated that Official Development Assistance (ODA) for education will fall by $3.2 billion, a 24% decrease from 2023 .

This case study examines the Justice and Stability in the Sahel (JASS) programme in Mali and Niger to explore whether climate action in the Sahel can generate broader peace and social benefits alongside enhanced resilience.
