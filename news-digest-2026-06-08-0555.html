CNBC Africa · 5 Jun 2026

CNBC Africa · 4 Jun 2026

In an interview discussing the latest rating action, Rafael Alberti, assistant vice president analyst at Moody’s, said the outlook change was driven primarily by a deterioration in Mali’s security environment over the past year, which the agency believes could threaten the stability of the transition government and, in turn, the sovereign’s ability to service its debt.
